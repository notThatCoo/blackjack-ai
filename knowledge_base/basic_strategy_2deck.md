# Basic Strategy — 2-Deck, Dealer Hits Soft 17 (H17)

## Why 2-Deck Is Different

With fewer decks in the shoe, the composition of the remaining deck changes more dramatically after each card is removed. This shifts a handful of borderline plays compared to 6-deck strategy. The house edge is also slightly lower — around 0.19% with perfect play vs ~0.5% in a 6-deck game.

---

## Key Differences from 6-Deck Strategy

These are the plays that change. Everything not listed here is identical to 6-deck H17.

### Hard Totals

| Situation | 6-Deck | 2-Deck |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Hard 11 vs Ace | H | D |
| Hard 9 vs 2 | H | D |

- **Hard 11 vs Ace**: In 2-deck you double. Fewer decks means the remaining deck is richer in 10-value cards relative to the cards already seen.
- **Hard 9 vs 2**: In 2-deck you double. Same logic — greater density of high cards makes this a profitable double.

### Soft Totals

| Situation | 6-Deck | 2-Deck |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Soft 18 (A,7) vs 2 | Ds | S |
| Soft 19 (A,8) vs 4,5,6 | S / Ds | Ds |

- **Soft 18 vs 2**: In 2-deck you stand rather than double. The composition effect makes doubling marginally negative here.

### Pairs

| Situation | 6-Deck | 2-Deck |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 4,4 vs 4 | H | P |
| 3,3 vs 2 | P | H |
| 2,2 vs 2 | P | H |

## When You'll See 2-Deck Games

Two-deck games are less common in large casinos but are offered in smaller venues or private rooms. They're attractive to card counters because:

1. The count becomes meaningful earlier in the shoe
2. Fewer cards need to be seen before the true count is reliable
3. Penetration matters more — a 2-deck game dealt 75% deep gives you far more high-count hands than a 6-deck shoe at the same penetration percentage

---

## Practical Note

If you sit down at a table and are unsure how many decks are in play:
- Ask the dealer — they will tell you
- Look at the shoe (a 6-deck shoe is visibly large and heavy)
- Check the table felt, which usually states the number of decks

When in doubt, default to 6-deck strategy. The differences are small and most borderline plays cost very little either way.
