# Bet Spread Guidelines

## What Is a Bet Spread?

A bet spread is the ratio between your minimum bet and your maximum bet. Card counters vary their bets based on the true count — betting small when the house has the edge, and large when the player has the edge.

Example: a 1-12 spread means you bet $10 at a neutral count and up to $120 when the true count is +5 or higher.

---

## Hi-Lo True Count to Bet Sizing

Each +1 in true count shifts the edge approximately +0.5% toward the player.

| True Count | Player Edge (approx) | Recommended Bet |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------|
| 0 or less | House has edge (~0.5%) | 1 unit (min bet) |
| +1        | Near break-even        | 1 unit |
| +2        | Player +0.5%           | 2 units |
| +3        | Player +1.0%           | 4 units |
| +4        | Player +1.5%           | 8 units |
| +5+       | Player +2.0%+          | 12 units |

A "unit" is your base bet — typically the table minimum.

---

## Bankroll Requirements

Betting too aggressively relative to your bankroll leads to ruin even with a positive edge.

| Spread | Minimum Recommended Bankroll |
|--------|------------------------------|
| 1-4    | 100 units |
| 1-8    | 200 units |
| 1-12   | 300–400 units |

**Example**: If your unit is $10 and you play a 1-12 spread, keep $3,000–$4,000 as your dedicated bankroll to stay below ~5% risk of ruin.

---

## Walking Up to a Table Mid-Shoe

When you arrive at a table mid-shoe, you don't know the count yet. Options:

1. **Back-count first** — stand behind the table and count without playing. Only sit down when the count goes positive. This is called "Wonging." It is highly effective but obvious and sometimes restricted by casinos.
2. **Start at min bet** — sit down and bet minimum until you have counted enough cards to have a reliable true count. Less efficient but less suspicious.
3. **Estimate from shoe depth** — if the shoe is almost done (small stack remaining, cut card visible), there is less value left. Consider waiting for a fresh shoe.

When joining a fresh shoe (just shuffled), the count starts at 0 and you start at minimum bet.

---

## Casino Heat and Bet Camouflage

Casinos watch for bet spreads that correlate with the count. Signs you are getting attention ("heat"):
- A pit boss standing at your table watching closely
- The dealer shuffling early (killing penetration)
- Being asked to flat bet or leave

Basic camouflage:
- Ramp bets up gradually rather than jumping from min to max in one hand
- Occasionally make a slightly larger bet at a neutral count
- At high counts, spread to two hands instead of one giant bet — same EV, less obvious
- Take occasional breaks and move tables

Card counting is not illegal. Casinos are private property, however, and can ask you to leave or restrict your bets.

---

## Fresh Shoe vs Mid-Shoe Strategy

**Fresh shoe**: Count is zero. Bet minimum. The shoe has to develop before you have an edge.

**Mid-shoe, positive count**: This is what you are looking for. The remaining decks are rich in 10s and Aces, the dealer busts more, and your blackjacks pay more often.

**Mid-shoe, deeply negative count**: The remaining deck is full of low cards. The dealer is less likely to bust. Bet minimum and consider leaving.
